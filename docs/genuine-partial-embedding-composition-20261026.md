# Composing full embeddings of genuine partial functions

26 October 2026. For full embeddings e:A→B and f:B→C of genuinely partial Option(Set)-valued structures, their composite maps whole source output values to the exact two-step image of the entire set, preserving both full relational reflection and the independent Option domain constructor. The proof uses `imageSet (f∘e) U = imageSet f (imageSet e U)` and `Option.map`, so undefined `none` never becomes defined-empty `some ∅`.

The full embedding structure has only one data-bearing vertex-map field and proposition-valued proof fields. Embeddings therefore agree as soon as their vertex maps agree. This extensionality simplifies the comparison of composed complete-mask relational embeddings with composed genuine-partial functional embeddings and supports subsequent Ramsey-arrow colouring transfer.

These results are about FULL embeddings, not the EHN positive original-partial projection, which deliberately does not reflect undefined inputs.
