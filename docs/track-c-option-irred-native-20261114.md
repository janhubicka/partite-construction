at the closed-free-cover predicate level, not merely the
collapsed-native case.

IsOptionClosedFreeIrreducible forbids any cover by two closed

all SOURCE-DEFINED function INPUTS. Defined-empty is an

the full encoded copy to lie in one side. The ambient
native free amalgam is not assumed legal.

genuine partial closed-free predicate and any future

sufficient to justify the native EHN restriction
