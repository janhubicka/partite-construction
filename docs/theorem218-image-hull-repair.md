# Theorem 2.18: bypassing the five-vertex generator obstruction

9 October 2026. Continuation of the `100 allthose` / `10 allthose` audit.
The original manuscript and all literal published predicates are unchanged.
This note concerns the explicitly named **closed-test** positive proof.

## Recovered boundary

PR #178 refutes the assertion that every closed U-irreducible structure
is generated by an ordinarily irreducible induced seed. Its five-vertex
model has root/output triples

    (0,1,2), (0,2,1), (1,3,0), (2,4,0), (3,4,0).

Vertices 3 and 4 must both occur in any generating set, and at least one
of 1 and 2 must occur as well. Neither pair {1,4} nor {2,3} lies in a
positive tuple. Thus the proposed universal seed hypothesis really fails.
The conditional theorem from #177 must not be used without that hypothesis.

This obstruction is separate from the literal Lemma 2.29 counterexample
in #168/#169 and the Definition 2.17(4b) obstruction in #179. The new
argument below bypasses the seed obstruction only; it does not retract
or identify the other two issues.

## Replacement lemma

Let S and B be U-closed relational structures, S be U-irreducible, and
f:S -> B be a positive relational homomorphism. Then

    H = B induced on cl_U^B(f[S])

is U-closed and U-irreducible. No finiteness, injectivity or reflection is
needed. In particular, **the raw image f[S] is not claimed closed or
induced**, and S is not claimed to have an ordinary irreducible seed.

More generally, any closed target generated by such a homomorphic image
is U-irreducible. Suppose the target freely decomposes into two closed
induced sides L and R. Their images are relative U-substructures because
both the sides and the target are closed. Positive homomorphisms pull
relative U-substructures back to relative U-substructures. Hence
f^{-1}(L) and f^{-1}(R) are closed induced substructures of S.

They cover all source vertices. Every source relation tuple maps to a
target relation tuple, which lies wholly in L or wholly in R; its source
tuple therefore lies wholly in the corresponding preimage. The source
is a free amalgam of these two closed preimages. U-irreducibility forces
one preimage to be all S. The image f[S] lies in that target side, and
target generation forces the side to be the entire target. This proves
the general statement. Apply it to H, which is generated by f[S].

The formal argument explicitly covers nullary relation tuples and empty
carriers through the existing `closed_cover` theorem. It does not assume
that the common root of an arbitrary decomposition is independently
closed, nor that all induced substructures of an irreducible object are
irreducible.

## Arbitrary positive coordinate powers

Let p:B -> A be a positive part map protecting all closed U-irreducible
tests, with B closed. Let P be the actual native tagged induced power
of B with exponent N>0. Given an embedded closed U-irreducible test S in
P, consider each coordinate evaluation f_k:S -> B.

By the replacement lemma, H_k=cl_U^B(f_k[S]) is a closed U-irreducible
induced substructure of B. Therefore p restricted to H_k is a full
embedding. This immediately supplies two facts for every coordinate k:

* Equal part labels of x,y in S imply f_k(x)=f_k(y).
* Every relation on the part labels reflects to that relation on f_k[S].

The first fact, in **all** coordinates, gives injectivity of the part
projection on S. The second, in **all** coordinates, gives reflection
in the coordinatewise power. Positivity of the power projection follows
from N>0 and ordinary partiteness, itself already a consequence of the
closed protected part map. Thus all positive native powers preserve
closed-test protection.

This is exactly the missing conclusion, not a weaker seed-restricted
substitute. It applies to tests such as the five-vertex counterexample.

The protected restriction map is already available from #176. Its
support is relatively U-closed, as the preimage of the embedded closed

## Suggested local proof wording (not applied to frozen manuscript)

For each coordinate, take the U-closure of the image of the whole
U-closed U-irreducible substructure. This closure is U-irreducible:
a free decomposition into U-closed sides pulls back to a free
decomposition of the source, and the side containing its entire image
contains its U-closure. The projection of the old partite system is an
embedding on this closure. Applying this at every coordinate establishes
injectivity and reflection of the part projection of the power.

This paragraph belongs only to a proof using the explicitly specified
closed-test convention. It must not be inserted as a proof of the
literal nonclosed-test assertion without a separate argument.

## What remains open

1. The complete boundary-aware rank-increment step, in particular the
   Picture histories whose generator attachments have no common
   Hales--Jewett parameter coordinate. This patch proves the local
   Ramsey/protection/coverage step, not small-rank completion preservation.
2. Iteration of that rank step and the final locally finite completion
   application for the positive theorem.
3. The semantic bridge to **literal** Definitions 2.15 and 2.17(4a)-(4c).
   Source closedness is essential in the replacement lemma. #179 shows
   why simply replacing intrinsic irreducibility by relative
   irreducibility does not, by itself, repair (4b). Any proposed
   correction must coordinate the definitions and Lemmas 2.28--2.31;
   it is not established merely by proving the closed-test theorem.

Accordingly, Theorem 2.18 is not marked as validated here.
