# Partite construction

Reusable Lean formalization of structural Ramsey theory from Appendix A of
*Twenty years of Nešetřil's classification programme of Ramsey classes*.

## Main endpoints

The relational non-induced and induced constructions are checked end to end.
`Partite.orderedRamsey` gives the unrestricted ordered relational theorem and
`Partite.Induced.inducedConstruction` gives the induced partite construction
with its irreducible-image trace.

The recursive Ramsey construction for genuinely set-valued functions is
checked in its positive-input-arity form as
`Structure.orderedClosedGraphRamsey`.  The constants issue is removed at the
EHN level by the canonical fixed-root construction described below.

The **functional EHN theorem for arbitrary function arities, including
constants**, is
`StructuralRamsey.Rooted.Structure.FreeAmalgamationClass.orderedRamsey_allArity`
in `Ramsey/FreeAmalgamationFunctionsAllArity.lean`.  It applies to hereditary
classes closed under full free amalgamation, with finite nonempty colour types;
neither A nor B is assumed irreducible and every embedding in the Ramsey arrow
is full/closed.

The exact survey theorem is
`Structure.orderedRamsey_forbidden_expansions_allArity` in
`Ramsey/ForbiddenFunctionsAllArity.lean`.  Forbidden structures may prescribe
particular orders, and only their order reducts need to be irreducible.  The
order-completion invariant prevents creation of new forbidden ordered copies.

There are two complementary constant-handling mechanisms, both formalized.
The rooted-language reduction removes the canonical root
`A.nullaryRoot = <empty>`, produces a moving language whose functions all
have positive arity, transports embeddings/free amalgams/classes, and
reconstructs the order.  Separately, the class-preserving EHN pass itself now
handles arbitrary arities directly: `FunctionalPartite.EHN.initial_allArity`
amalgamates the initial B-copies over their canonical nullary root, and
`FunctionalPartite.EHN.inducedConstruction_allArity` performs the usual one
induced refinement pass.

The **strict relational sparsening theorem** with B irreducible and arbitrary
A is
`Partite.IteratedSparsening.sparseningRamsey_strict_baseIrreducible_all`.
It bypasses the stronger synchronized tree invariant rather than proving it.

The **direct positive-arity functional n-pass construction** (without U-closed
relational stages or repeated recursive Ramsey work) is
`Structure.inducedRamsey_nativeFunctionalIteration_originalPartial_all`.
The stronger **corrected functional sparsening with final B-copy coverage**
is `Structure.sparseningRamsey_nativeFunctional_graphStrict_withBCover` in
`Functional/NativeIteratedFinalBCover.lean`. It preserves the full Ramsey
arrow, the original 2019 partial-function (EHN) global projection, strict
**relational function-graph** B-tree completions on every n-vertex weak test,
and extension of every irreducible to a genuine full B-copy. It does **not**
assert strict full-functional local B-trees. The native closed common
separator has a one-copy strict original-partial completion, but compatible
relative extensions over the same reducible separator remain to be proved.

The published Appendix A Picture induction is now available on one genuine
functional witness, not as independent existence proofs:
`FunctionalPartite.EHN.initial_weakTree_and_projectedCover`,
`FunctionalPartite.EHN.pictureLemma_weakTree_and_projectedCover`, and
`FunctionalPartite.EHN.inducedConstruction_weakTree_and_projectedCover`.
These are covered by the CI/axiom-verified merged PR #273. The exported
`Structure.sparseningRamsey_nativeFunctional_graphStrict_withBCover_unrestricted`
removes the ambient free-amalgamation class parameter. All four preserve the
exact weak-substructure vertex budget and concern strict **function-graph**
B-tree targets, not unproved strict full-functional local B-trees.

## Strict original-partial function-tree results (verified)

The direct weak-function-GRAPH n-pass theorem above remains the checked
vertex-exact result. For the stronger **strict full-functional B-tree**
target in the **original 2019 partial-function map convention**, the
following separate reusable endpoints are now proved and axiom-audited:

- `Structure.IsFreeAmalgam.originalPartialTreeCompletion_glue_relative`:
  compatible relative strict B-tree witnesses glue over one prescribed
  (possibly reducible) strict root tree; the root map may be noninjective.
- `Structure.HasOriginalPartialTreeCompletion.of_EHN_generator_image_drop`:
  for a genuine closed test generated by G, fewer than n projected
  generators suffice for strict local completion from the preceding
  rank-(n−1) invariant, even if the target function-closed hull has
  more than n vertices.
- `Structure.IsFreeAmalgam.one_generated_no_mixed`: a genuine full free
  amalgam generated by at most one vertex is contained in one side.
- `FunctionalPartite.EHN.sparsening_originalPartial_strict_one_all`:
  **unconditional rank-one** native functional sparsening with full
  Ramsey embeddings, original-partial projection, strict full-function
  local B-trees of every genuine closed test on ≤1 vertex, and final
  genuine B-cover, **without** the artificial A↪B assumption (B
  irreducible and positive function arity remain in the hypotheses).
- `FunctionalPartite.EHN.StrictRanked.sparsening_of_maximal_mixed_schema`:
  the complete **n-pass** native functional Ramsey construction and
  final strict original-partial B-coverage are proved **conditional**
  on one explicit uniform `MaximalMixedSchema` hypothesis.

**The uniform mixed-root hypothesis is too strong, not merely unproved.**
Its quantification over arbitrary EHN old/core stages includes the explicit
finite graph five-cycle in
[docs/maximal-mixed-hard-root-schema-counterexample.md](docs/maximal-mixed-hard-root-schema-counterexample.md).
The graph and rank certificates have a finite executable regression;
the complete Stage-level counterexample is not yet Lean encoded.
The checked n-pass theorem remains a valid implication, but it is NOT an
unconditional proof strategy with this false universal premise. A viable
replacement must carry actual Hales--Jewett Picture histories (or stronger
root compatibility) through the weak-substructure iteration.
Do not confuse original-partial projections with the separately refuted
total-fibre global homomorphism.

These proof modules are available through the `PartiteConstruction`
umbrella import. Full details and exact scope:
[docs/appendix-a.md](docs/appendix-a.md).

## Further verified mixed-root reductions

The precise remaining step for strict original-2019 full-functional B-tree
completion is compatible **relative** target-tree extensions of the two
sides of an actual maximal-rank mixed native attachment. The following
kernel-checked tools now reduce this problem:

- A full-functional separator generated by at most **one vertex** is
  irreducible even if it contains many function-generated vertices.
  The actual native EHN theorem
  `Attachment.native_closedTest_oneGeneratedRoot_compatibleRelativeCompletions`
  constructs BOTH relative witnesses over the same Base-copy.
- `IsFreeAmalgam.left_generatedByAtMost_of_exterior_root_seeds` proves
  that the generators of a root can be paid for by an equally large
  reserve of source generators outside a closed side, preventing any
  increase in the generator rank of that side.
- `IsFreeAmalgam.compatibleOriginalPartialRelativeCompletions_of_exterior_root_budget`
  synchronizes absolute strict completions over an **irreducible**
  root when exterior reserves pay for its generators on both sides.
- `StrictRanked.sparsening_of_hard_root_schema_from_two_all` proves the
  ENTIRE functional n-pass Ramsey and final B-copy construction
  conditional only on genuinely mixed maximal-rank native tests
  whose common separator needs **at least two generators**.
  This sharpest fully checked conditional endpoint requires no
  A↪B initialization: if A does not embed into B, simply use C=B.

**Do not attempt to prove the universal hard-root schema as stated.**
The five-cycle audit refutes it even with irreducible Base and two strict
forest stages. The unresolved *actual* task is a history-sensitive
maximal-image weak-test lemma for the genuine function-language HJ Picture,
not arbitrary full support attachments. The corrected induction must count
exact weak vertices and use compatible relative completions over one root.
Per-fibre output transversality is retained throughout. The later survey's
total-fibre global homomorphism remains a separate, refuted interpretation.
## Weak-restriction conventions and the functional induction

Two weak restrictions are now kept distinct. `Structure.weakInduce` retains
each surviving output, so its fibre is `F(x) ∩ S`. The tagged singleton
closure and `USize` dictionary uses this convention. In contrast,
`PartialSetStructure.wholeWeakInduce` makes the entire application undefined
if any output leaves `S`. Its `Option (Set V)` values retain the difference
between undefined and defined-empty. For `F(a) = {b,c}` and `S = {a,b}`,
the first restriction keeps `{b}` and the second is undefined.

The whole-fibre API proves full embedding restriction on exact preimages,
source-defined homomorphism restriction to exact weak images, and full
free-amalgam decomposition on exact weak sides and their common root.
`Structure.IsFreeAmalgam.wholeWeakPartialInduce_withMaps` applies this
decomposition to the existing native construction with its original
nonempty-defined convention. No tested set is replaced by its closure.

`PartialSetStructure.wholeValueGraph` records an entire finite output value
in one relation tuple. Its induced restriction is exactly the graph of the
whole-fibre weak restriction, including defined-empty values. This is not
yet a canonical `U`-closure description: repeated output enumerations are
allowed, and the ambient active-root data still needs to be incorporated.
The older singleton-output dictionary must not be transferred to this
convention without proof. Compatible strict target-tree completions in the
actual mixed Hales--Jewett step also remain open.

See [the whole-fibre restriction proof](docs/whole-fibre-weak-restriction-20261020.md),
[the exact free-amalgam cut](docs/whole-fibre-weak-free-amalgam-20261020.md), and
[the complete-value tuple encoding](docs/whole-output-tuple-weak-restriction-20261020.md).

## Map conventions

`Structure.IsHomomorphism` retains equality of complete function-value
images.  `Structure.IsEHNHomomorphismEmbedding` uses weak preservation
globally and full embeddings on full irreducibles.  The older
`Structure.IsWeakHomomorphismEmbedding` is the graph-encoding notion used by
weak-substructure iteration; it remains a distinct interface.

## Build and audit

```sh
set -e
lake exe cache get Mathlib.Tactic Mathlib.Data.Fintype.Pi Mathlib.Data.Fintype.Basic Mathlib.Data.Finset.Dedup
lake build
lake env lean CheckAxioms.lean > axioms.log
lake env lean CheckClosureAxioms.lean >> axioms.log
lake env lean CheckEHNFunctions.lean >> axioms.log
python3 scripts/check_axioms.py axioms.log CheckAxioms.lean CheckClosureAxioms.lean CheckEHNFunctions.lean
```

CI builds the umbrella and the audit imports.  Only `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` are accepted; missing audit output,
`sorryAx`, and custom unproved axioms fail.
